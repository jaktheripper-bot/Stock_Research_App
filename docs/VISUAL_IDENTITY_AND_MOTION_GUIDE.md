# MOATNOMICS — Visual Identity & Motion Guide (Version 2.0)

> **Version 2.0 | 20 October 2026**
> **Creative Territory:** *Less noise, more nuance.*
> **Core Principle:** *Nuance should make decisions clearer, not make the reader work harder.*
> **Final Operating Rule:** *Make the evidence easier to understand, not easier to believe.*

A cross-channel working standard for brand identity, product UI, research reports, editorial content, video, motion, multilingual production, and quality control.

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Approved direction** | An explicit brand-owner decision to preserve unless deliberately reopened. |
| **Provisional hypothesis** | A recommended starting point that must be tested and approved before becoming a final specification. |
| **Production requirement** | A process, accessibility, evidence-integrity, or delivery rule teams must follow. |
| **Open decision** | An unresolved issue that must not be silently treated as approved. |

> [!NOTE]
> This guide improves production readiness. It does not claim final vector artwork, licensed fonts, contrast certification, or completed user research. These remain release gates.

## 0. How to Use Version 2.0

This is a balanced operating manual for product UI, research/report design, editorial content, and all video formats. It is tool-agnostic and designed for multilingual production.

| Decision for v2.0 | Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Priority: balanced** | Comparable attention to product, reports, content, and video. |
| **Video formats: all-video** | Short-form, YouTube, explainers, webinars, product demos, presentations, and brand films. |
| **Product theme: both** | Light and dark are first-class themes with separate contrast testing. |
| **Motion workflow: tool-agnostic** | Specify purpose, timing, layers, easing, output, and acceptance criteria rather than software steps. |
| **Language strategy: multilingual** | Plan for script coverage, text expansion, subtitles, voice, localisation, and right-to-left readiness. |

### 0.1 Approved Direction vs. Hypotheses
- Preserve **editorial authority combined with hidden perspective**, a composed/reassuring quality, and a symbol plus wordmark.
- **Logo Direction (Approved):** The chosen logo direction is the **earlier sophisticated dimensional split-arch with a central mirror** (Reference Boards A & C). **Do not replace it with the later perfect-semicircle revision (Reference Board B) without approval.**
- *"Less noise, more nuance."* is the working tagline territory, not a market-validated claim.
- Palette, type, exact logo dimensions, grid values, and motion ranges remain provisional until tested.
- When a spec is missing, record an assumption and decision owner rather than inventing approval.

### 0.2 Core Principle
**Nuance should make decisions clearer, not make the reader work harder.**
Moatnomics helps investors look beyond the obvious by bringing evidence, context, and alternative perspectives into view. Rigorous without being cold; distinctive without being theatrical; calm without being passive.

### Product Journey & Design Job

| Journey | User Question | Design Job |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discover** | *Why look at this?* | Make the central question and relevance clear. |
| **Investigate** | *What is the evidence?* | Expose source, date, method, and assumptions. |
| **Evaluate / compare** | *What are the trade-offs?* | Align scales, labels, and assumptions for fair comparison. |
| **Monitor** | *What changed, and does it matter?* | Separate meaningful changes from routine movement. |

- **Investigate deeply:** Guide from headline to evidence; never hide complexity.
- **Challenge independently:** Give risks and counterarguments visual dignity.
- **Trace the evidence:** Make source, as-of date, method, and uncertainty findable.
- **Monitor what changes:** Explain what changed, why it matters, and what could invalidate the view.
- **Guardrail:** Never let visual polish imply more certainty, verification, or completeness than the underlying work supports.

## 2. Logo System: The Perspective Portal

The **split arch** creates an opening; the **central mirrored vertical plane** suggests depth and another vantage. The wordmark supplies editorial authority. The symbol must remain recognisable when dimensional treatment is removed.

- **A Broader Perspective:** A split arch with a central mirror creates distance, revealing a clearer, wider view.
- **The Mirror:** Reflects a different angle, representing deeper context and multiple perspectives.
- **Depth and Discovery:** The open form suggests a look through — beyond the obvious.

### 2.1 Master Artwork & Construction Rules
- Build final masters as editable vector paths. Required deliverables: SVG, print-ready PDF, transparent PNG exports, and a native vector source.
- Draw from the **chosen sophisticated split-arch concept** (with angled inner perspective planes and a taller central vertical mirror prism), not from a raster mock-up. Keep the mirror centred and the left/right mass optically balanced.
- Create `dimensional`, `flat`, `simplified small-size`, `reverse`, `black`, `white`, and `one-colour` variants. **The flat master is the default for UI, favicon, and monochrome use.**
- Large brand imagery may use two or three mirror planes; the core logo must stay simple and reproducible.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, outline, add unapproved shadows, independently recolour logo parts, or add lens flares/glossy chrome.
- Do not place the mark over busy imagery without a quiet zone or tested contrast treatment. **Never use image generation as the production logo master.**

| Rule | Starting Hypothesis | Validation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clear space** | At least central-mirror width on every side. | Test horizontal, stacked, and symbol-only lockups. |
| **Digital symbol** | `16 px` initial test point. | Test at `16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48 px` across themes. |
| **Digital lockup** | `140 px` minimum width initial test. | Check wordmark legibility; omit tagline when cramped. |
| **Print symbol** | `6 mm` initial test point. | Test office print and photocopy. |
| **Print lockup** | `30 mm` width initial test. | Check wordmark/descriptor at actual output size. |

| Context | Use | Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product header** | Horizontal lockup where space allows; otherwise symbol if context is clear. | No tagline in cramped navigation. |
| **Report cover** | Primary lockup. | Title/date/topic remain dominant. |
| **Report interior / dense UI** | Small flat symbol or wordmark. | Never compete with data or sources. |
| **Favicon / app tile** | Simplified flat symbol. | No mirror detail that collapses. |
| **Dark surface** | Approved reverse/light variant. | No ad hoc recolouring. |
| **Mono / photocopy** | Flat one-colour master. | Preserve negative-space logic. |
| **Video end card** | Mobile-readable lockup. | Tagline only with adequate dwell time. |

## 3. Colour System and Themes

These values are provisional design hypotheses, not certified combinations. Test text, controls, focus rings, icons, charts, and print output before approval.

| Token | Provisional Hex | Role / Guardrail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forest** | `#0E3D32` | Brand anchor and primary action candidate; not synonymous with success. |
| **Sage** | `#7DA77A` | Support accent/series; test small-text contrast. |
| **Lime** | `#C7E268` | Rare focal point; not automatically profit/success. |
| **Ink Navy** | `#0B2337` | Editorial authority, body text, and axes. |
| **Ivory** | `#F7F5EE` | Warm page/report surface. |
| **Mist** | `#D9E3E8` | Dividers, neutral panels, and secondary series. |
| **Charcoal** | `#282F33` | Sustained reading and dense text. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Utility neutral and reverse surfaces. |

### Dual-Theme Mapping (Light & Dark as First-Class Themes)

| Role | Light Theme Hypothesis | Dark Theme Hypothesis | Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas** | `Ivory` (`#F7F5EE`) or `White` (`#FFFFFF`). | `Ink Navy` (`#0B2337`) or deep `Forest` (`#0E3D32`). | Define canvas and elevated surface separately. |
| **Primary text** | `Ink Navy` (`#0B2337`) / `Charcoal` (`#282F33`). | `Ivory` (`#F7F5EE`) / validated near-white. | Do not simply invert every token. |
| **Secondary text** | Tested dark neutral. | Tested light neutral (`Mist` `#D9E3E8`). | Metadata and sources remain readable. |
| **Borders** | `Mist` (`#D9E3E8`) or tested neutral. | Dedicated lighter dark-surface border. | Boundaries remain visible. |
| **Primary action** | `Forest` (`#0E3D32`) fill with tested light label. | Validated high-contrast action token (`Lime` `#C7E268` with dark label or `Forest` with light border). | Same action hierarchy in both themes. |
| **Focal accent** | Small `Lime` (`#C7E268`) highlight. | Small `Lime` (`#C7E268`) highlight. | Never use as large glow or sole status cue. |
| **Charts** | Neutral grid, explicit labels (`Forest`, `Sage`, `Mist`, `Ink`). | Theme-adjusted neutral grid. | Test series independently in each theme. |

### 3.1 Semantic Status Tokens

| Token | Required Behaviour |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Positive / favourable** | Dedicated token plus label/icon; distinct from generic brand green (`Forest`/`Lime`). |
| **Negative / adverse** | Dedicated token plus text, sign, or line style; never red/green alone. |
| **Caution** | Distinct from error and ordinary highlight. |
| **Error** | High-salience state with actionable recovery copy. |
| **Information** | Neutral explanatory state, not a recommendation. |
| **Focus** | Visible focus ring in both themes. |
| **Disabled / selected** | Clear non-colour cue; retain readable text where possible. |

## 4. Typography and Multilingual Readiness

| Role | Direction | Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display / editorial** | Serif, **Tiempos Headline** / **Playfair Display**-like direction only. | Major titles and selected campaign headlines; verify licence and script coverage. |
| **Product / body** | Neutral sans, **Inter**-like direction only. | UI, body, labels, tables, and annotations. |
| **Numeric / data** | Sans with **tabular figures (`tnum`)**. | Prices, returns, ratios, dates, and aligned comparisons. |
| **Micro-labels** | Same sans family, restrained tracking. | Short metadata only; avoid uppercase tracking in long translated copy. |

| Type Token | Starting Range | Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display XL** | `40–56 px` responsive | Campaign / landing headline. |
| **H1** | `30–40 px` | Page / report title. |
| **H2** | `22–28 px` | Major section. |
| **H3** | `17–21 px` | Subsection / card title. |
| **Body** | `15–18 px` web; `10–11 pt` report | Sustained reading; validate density. |
| **Compact body** | `13–14 px` web | Dense tables only after testing. |
| **Caption / metadata** | `12–14 px` web; `8–9 pt` print | Sources/dates must remain legible. |

### 4.1 Multilingual Rules
- Choose fonts only after testing all intended scripts, punctuation, currencies (`₹`, `$`, `€`), numerals, combining marks, and fallback behaviour.
- Use locale-aware date, number, percentage, currency, and unit formatting. Preserve source currency and reporting period when financially material.
- Stress-test **30–40% text expansion** as a planning scenario. Keep critical text editable; allow line wraps and component heights to grow.
- Support RTL handling where in scope; **do not mirror the logo by default**.

## 5. Layout and Responsive Behaviour

| Element | Starting Hypothesis | Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spacing** | `4 px` scale. | Use semantic tokens, not arbitrary one-off values. |
| **Desktop grid** | `12 columns`. | Record max width, gutters, and breakpoints in tokens. |
| **Mobile grid** | `4 columns`. | Let research content reflow; do not compress desktop tables indiscriminately. |
| **Line length** | About `60–80 characters` where practical. | Prioritise reading; data tables may be wider. |
| **Touch target** | Aim for `44 × 44 px` where feasible. | Separate hit area from `20–24 px` artwork. |
| **Radii** | Small restrained family. | Provisional until tested; do not round every element equally. |

- Align headings, body, charts, source notes, and cards to a shared grid.
- **Use whitespace before adding boxes.** Keep source notes close to the claim/chart they qualify.
- At narrow widths, reorder only if the reading logic remains correct; never detach units, legends, or caveats from charts.
- Test 200% zoom and larger text without clipping critical information.

## 6. Graphic Language, Imagery and Texture

- **The Portal Motif:** Use layered portal forms, perspective crops, vertical split-plane masks, rules, and framed reveals to express *looking through to context* (`DEPTH`, `PERSPECTIVE`, `CLARITY`, `OPPORTUNITY`). **The portal is a signature device, not a mandatory background.**
- **Where to Use Portal Art vs. Flat Surfaces:**
  - **Use portal art / atmospheric landscape reveals for:** Campaign heroes, homepage hero, report covers, section openers, and selected video transitions.
  - **Keep flat:** Dense UI tables, screeners, watchlists, source lists, and report interiors.
- **One Dominant Perspective Gesture:** Do not stack portal, gradient, texture, chart, and photography without a clear hierarchy.
- **Photographic & Atmospheric Direction:** Prefer depth, natural atmospheric landscapes (misty mountain valleys, calm reflective water, architectural split-arch monoliths catching warm sunlight), and a clear focal point. Grade toward deep greens (`Forest` `#0E3D32`), warm neutrals (`Ivory` `#F7F5EE`), and restrained cool shadows (`Ink Navy` `#0B2337` / `Mist` `#D9E3E8`).
- **Anti-Cliché Rule:** Do not green-wash every image or use generic skyscrapers/stock-ticker screens as a substitute for explanation. Research visuals must not imply a false location, event, company, or investment context.

## 7. Iconography and Illustration

| Rule | Working Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grid** | `24 × 24 px` starting grid; optical adjustments at `16 / 20 px`. |
| **Stroke** | `1.75–2 px` at `24 px` as a starting hypothesis; test at real size. |
| **Geometry** | Consistent joins, corner language, and optical weight (`Ink Navy` `#0B2337` / `Forest` `#0E3D32` on light; `Ivory` `#F7F5EE` / `Sage` `#7DA77A` on dark). |
| **Metaphors** | Familiar, unambiguous; do not force the portal motif into every icon. Zero raw OS emojis in production UI. |
| **Accessibility** | Icon-only controls need accessible names, visible focus, and `44×44 px` hit areas; status icons need text/another cue. |

### Canonical Icon Metaphor Map

| Function | Metaphor | Caution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Research dossier** | Document + magnifying glass (`Research`) | Not a generic download. |
| **Markets** | Ascending bar/trend chart (`Markets`) | Label asset class if ambiguous. |
| **Compare** | Overlapping circles / side-by-side sliders (`Compare`) | Keep semantics consistent. |
| **Watchlist** | Bookmark ribbon (`Watchlist`) | Differentiate from alerts and saved reports. |

## 8. Data Visualisation and Research Graphics

- Choose the simplest chart that accurately expresses the relationship. Label axes, units, periods, currency, benchmark, and frequency where relevant.
- Show source and as-of date consistently. Label estimated, modelled, forecast, and scenario values explicitly.
- Use labels, line styles, markers, or annotations in addition to colour (`Forest` `#0E3D32`, `Sage` `#7DA77A`, `Mist` `#D9E3E8`, `Ink Navy` `#0B2337`, focal `Lime` `#C7E268` ring callout). **Avoid 3D charts, neon glow, ornamental grids, and misleading truncated axes.**
- Never use `Lime` / `Green` automatically to signal gain or recommendation. Do not hide counterevidence or invalidation conditions through visual hierarchy.

### 8.1 Standard 6-Part Chart Anatomy
1. **Question / insight title**, proportionate to evidence (e.g., *"Global Equities — Long-term value creation"*).
2. **Subtitle** with period, scope, benchmark, or key assumption.
3. **Plot** with clear scales, units, and series distinction.
4. **Annotation callout** explaining the relevant change or comparison (e.g., `Ink Navy` / `Forest` callout box pointing to an inflection point: *"A broader perspective reveals a different story."*).
5. **Source, as-of date, and methodology note.**
6. **Caveat or scenario label** where needed.

## 9. Digital Product UI: Light and Dark

- **Functional clarity outranks brand decoration.** Keep dense tables, source lists, and watchlists free of cinematic backgrounds.
- Brand colours do not replace semantic status colours.
- Use portal motif sparingly in empty states, onboarding, or a simplified loading cue only when it does not delay work.
- Make uncertainty legible in labels and explanation, not vague colour or opacity. **Missing/stale data must never silently become zero or an inferred value.**

| Component | States to Design | Acceptance Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buttons** | Default, hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading | Primary: `Forest` (`#0E3D32`) fill on light / `Lime` (`#C7E268`) on dark hero; Secondary: clean `Mist`/`Ink` outline. |
| **Inputs** | Empty, filled, focused, error, disabled, read-only | Persistent label; recoverable error copy; `>=16px` mobile font size. |
| **Tabs / navigation** | Default, selected, focus, disabled | Selected state uses pill fill (`Lime` `#C7E268` or `Forest` `#0E3D32`) + text weight, not colour-only. |
| **Tables** | Sort, filter, selected row, empty, loading, error | Tabular numerals (`tnum`); headers/units remain attached. |
| **Charts** | Loading, no data, valid, partial, error, stale | Missing/stale data disclosed, not shown as zero. |
| **Alerts** | Info, caution, adverse, resolved, muted | State, consequence, evidence, and action clear. |
| **Dialogs / panels** | Open, close, focus, validation, escape | Top-layer portaling outside `<main inert>`, focus order, and return focus defined. |

## 10. Research Reports and Presentations (PDF Export)

1. **Cover hierarchy:** Lockup (`The Perspective Portal`), report title (Tiempos/Playfair serif), asset/sector/topic, publication or as-of date, and circular/split-arch perspective landscape graphic on `Ivory` (`#F7F5EE`) or `Forest` (`#0E3D32`) surface.
2. **Interior structure:**
   - Executive conclusion and key takeaways
   - What changed and why it matters
   - Evidence, methodology, and source quality
   - Business, asset, or instrument analysis
   - Valuation / scenarios / comparison basis
   - Risks, counterarguments, and invalidation conditions
   - Assumptions, limitations, source list, and as-of date
3. **Rule:** Keep dimensional mirror imagery on covers or special openers; **body pages and tables stay flat** on `Ivory` (`#F7F5EE`) or `White` (`#FFFFFF`).

## 11. Motion Identity

Motion enacts the brand idea: **the view opens, layers separate, context appears, and the conclusion becomes clearer.**
- **Reveal, do not explode:** Masking, restrained translation, measured parallax, or opacity change.
- **Calm is not slow:** UI feedback must feel immediate. One dominant motion idea per sequence.
- **Reduced motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; never make information available only through animation.

| Motion Class | Starting Duration | Behaviour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micro feedback** | `100–180 ms` | Ease-out, minimal travel. |
| **UI state** | `160–240 ms` | Ease-out / in-out; responsiveness first. |
| **Card / panel** | `220–360 ms` | Modest travel, preserve orientation. |
| **Chart reveal** | `350–700 ms` | Editorial explanation, not every refresh. |
| **Logo reveal** | `700–1,200 ms` | Brand film / formal opener only. |
| **Title / end card** | `600–1,000 ms` | Soft settle, no bounce. |
| **Section transition** | `300–700 ms` | Only if it preserves orientation. |

### 11.2 Master Logo Reveal (4 Beats)
1. **Opening (`0–250 ms`):** Arch halves emerge through quiet mask/vertical reveal and settle.
2. **Perspective (`200–550 ms`):** Mirror appears via restrained luminance shift/reflected band; no lens flare.
3. **Wordmark (`450–800 ms`):** Wordmark arrives gently after symbol resolves.
4. **Hold (`800–1,200 ms`):** Tagline only if screen/time allow; briefly hold full lockup.

## 12. Video System & Narrative Grammar

**8-Step Video Narrative Grammar:**
1. **Question:** What are we trying to understand?
2. **Context:** What must viewers know first?
3. **Evidence:** Source, period, units, and comparison.
4. **Interpretation:** What the evidence supports and does not.
5. **Counterpoint:** Risk, alternative explanation, or limitation.
6. **Implication:** What matters for understanding.
7. **Watchpoint:** What future evidence could change the view.
8. **Source and brand close:** Provenance and next steps.
