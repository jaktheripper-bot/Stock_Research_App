1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHAT THIS SITE IS & WHY IT EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Moatnomics is an institutional-grade, multi-asset financial research and forensic auditing software utility built specifically for Indian capital market allocators, self-directed investors, family offices, and corporate treasuries.

Most retail and semi-institutional platforms suffer from two structural flaws:
  1. Single-Asset Myopia: They evaluate equities in isolation without comparing a company's equity free-cash-flow yield against its own senior secured bonds, commercial REIT distributions, or the Indian 10-Year Sovereign G-Sec risk-free benchmark.
  2. AI Hallucination vs. Static Screeners: Legacy screeners dump raw financial ratios without qualitative business context, while generic AI chat wrappers hallucinate numbers, misread Indian accounting footnotes (Ind AS), and ignore statutory Indian tax laws (Sections 112A, 50AA, 115UA, 47(viic)).

Moatnomics solves both problems by pairing a Deterministic Quantitative Engine (which computes all financial ratios, bond duration/convexity, DuPont decompositions, Ind AS 108 segment disaggregations, forensic accounting sieves, and tax waterfalls deterministically before any prose is generated) with Grounded Multi-Agent AI Synthesis restricted to explaining pre-verified, primary-source figures under strict 4-tier epistemic separation.
