1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHAT THIS SITE IS & WHY IT EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Moatnomics is an institutional-grade, multi-asset financial research and forensic auditing platform built specifically for Indian capital market allocators, self-directed investors, family offices, and corporate treasuries.

Most retail and semi-institutional platforms suffer from two fatal flaws:
  1. Single-Asset Myopia: They evaluate equities in isolation without comparing a company's equity free-cash-flow yield against its own senior secured bonds, commercial REIT distributions, or the Indian 10-Year Sovereign G-Sec risk-free benchmark.
  2. AI Hallucination vs. Static Screeners: Legacy screeners dump raw financial ratios without qualitative context, while generic AI chat wrappers hallucinate numbers, misread Indian accounting footnotes (Ind-AS), and ignore statutory Indian tax laws (Sections 112A, 50AA, 115UA, 47(viic)).

Moatnomics solves both problems by pairing a Deterministic Quantitative Engine (which computes all financial math, bond duration/convexity, DuPont decompositions, forensic accounting sieves, and tax waterfalls deterministically before any text is generated) with Grounded Multi-Agent AI Synthesis (which is restricted to explaining pre-verified, primary-source numbers).


2. CORE ADVANTAGES OF USING THE PLATFORM (THE VALUE PROPOSITION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Cross-Asset Capital Hierarchy Intelligence:
   Instead of siloed asset tabs, the platform links 8 distinct Indian asset classes into a single capital hierarchy:

   - RBI Sovereign Gold Bonds (All 36 active secondary-market SGB tranches)
   - Unlisted MSMEs & Emerging SMEs (Ministry of MSME Udyam Gazette enterprises linked to listed NSE/BSE corporate buyers)

B. Zero-Hallucination "Math First, Narrative Second" Architecture:
   The AI layer never invents financial ratios, yields, or valuations. Twelve deterministic analytical engines run against primary statutory data first, compressing raw filings by ~92.4% into verified mathematical packets before any narrative synthesis occurs.

C. Post-Tax, Post-Inflation "In-Hand" Realism:
   Nominal yields are an illusion. The platform natively models Indian statutory taxation (Finance Act 2023/2024 Sections 50AA, 112A, 111A, 115UA, and 47(viic)) alongside MOSPI Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation across 5 marginal tax brackets (0%, 10%, 20%, 30%, and 39% Peak HNI) so users see their true net real purchasing power return.

D. Sub-Second Rendering Speed with Live Exchange Grounding:
   Pre-computed institutional dossiers render in under 35 milliseconds, while live Level-2 order book depth and corporate announcements automatically trigger surgical updates whenever material changes occur.

  - Compresses 400+ pages of annual reports, quarterly exchange filings, and governance disclosures into a 60-second quantitative cockpit plus a deep-dive 7-pillar narrative—identifying accounting red flags and valuation traps before capital is deployed.

Reasons to Believe (Data, Analysis & Backend Perspective):
  1. Primary Exchange & Statutory Data Grounding:
     - Live market quotes, 52-week ranges, and 5-tier Level-2 bid/ask order books are ingested directly from official exchange feeds alongside direct BSE corporate filings and historical XBRL financial statements.
  2. The 10-Point Forensic Clean-Room Sieve:
     - Every equity is scored (0-100) across 10 deterministic accounting integrity checks before qualification:
       (1) Cash Realization: CFO / EBITDA >= 0.35 (flags paper profits unbacked by operating cash).
       (2) Statutory Tax Wedge: Effective Tax Rate (Tax / PBT) >= 15.0% (flags artificial tax-haven or deferred-tax earnings inflation).
       (3) Promoter Pledge Velocity: Total Promoter Pledge <= 20.0% and QoQ delta < 3.0%.
       (4) Auditor Stability: <= 1 auditor change in trailing 36 months (penalizes mid-term SEBI LODR Reg 30 resignations).
       (5) Contingent Liabilities Drag: Off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities < 50.0% of tangible net worth.
       (6) Related-Party Transaction (RPT) Leakage: Total RPT volume < 15.0% of consolidated revenue.
       (7) Receivables vs. Sales Divergence: DSO expansion < 30 days YoY (detects channel stuffing).
       (8) Debt Service & Solvency: Interest Coverage Ratio (EBIT / Interest) >= 1.75x and Debt-to-Equity <= 2.0x.
       (9) Tangible Capital Preservation: Strictly positive tangible net worth (rejects negative net worth deficits).
       (10) Institutional Liquidity Floor: Market capitalization >= Rs. 25 Crores.
  3. 3-Stage DuPont ROE & Cash Conversion Cycle Decomposition:
     - Mathematically decomposes Return on Equity into Net Profit Margin x Asset Turnover x Equity Multiplier (Total Assets / Equity) to prove whether ROE growth comes from genuine operating efficiency or hazardous debt leverage. Computes DIO + DSO - DPO Cash Conversion Cycles.
  4. Triangulated Valuation Radar:
     - Computes intrinsic fair value by blending three distinct models: 5-Year Historical Median P/E (35% weight), 2-Stage Conservative Discounted Cash Flow / DCF (40% weight), and Graham Earnings Power Value / EPV (25% weight) to derive a deterministic Margin of Safety %.
  5. Sector-Native Scoring Sieves:
     - Replaces generic ratios with sector-specific math (e.g., evaluating Banks/NBFCs on Net Interest Margin, RoA, and GNPA/NNPA ratios rather than manufacturing Debt-to-Equity; evaluating IT Services on FCF conversion and ROCE).
  6. 60-Day Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD) & Technical Overlay:
     - Benchmarks current price against 50-DMA/200-DMA and 60-day post-earnings drift bands to diagnose institutional accumulation vs. distribution drift and behavioral Disposition Effect traps.
  7. Event-Driven Surgical Cache Invalidation:
     - While dossiers are cached for instant delivery, an event-driven material change gate continuously monitors live prices and BSE corporate disclosures. If a stock moves >= 5% or files a material regulatory announcement, the affected pillar is surgically invalidated and re-synthesized.
