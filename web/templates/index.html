Engine Architecture • Four Research Disciplines

The Four Disciplines Behind Every Report

From raw statutory ingestion to closed-form Python math and multi-agent AI verification, every dossier is synthesized through four linked engineering disciplines.

Competence 01 • Data Provenance Primary Filings Only

Statutory Exchange & Registry Ingestion

Direct automated ingestion of BSE/NSE corporate filings, RBI primary auction cut-offs, CCIL Zero-Coupon Yield Curves, AMFI scheme disclosures, and Udyam statutory records—eliminating third-party aggregator drift and unverified consensus estimates.

Competence 02 • Quantitative Engine Closed-Form Math

Deterministic Financial & Risk Modelling

AI never computes or approximates numbers. Every valuation and risk metric is executed in hard Python solvers: 32-iteration bisection Reverse-DCF implied growth, 3-step DuPont ROE decomposition, Macaulay/Modified duration & convexity shock matrices, and Section 115UA tax waterfalls.

Competence 03 • Grounded AI Layer Multi-Agent Forensic RAG

Glass-Box Multi-Agent AI & Research Desk

Specialized AI agents read 100+ page statutory footnotes, auditor qualifications, related-party disclosures, and credit rating rationales—cross-examining management claims against hard cash-flow statements and powering our live interactive Research Desk.

Competence 04 • Epistemic Rigour Falsifiable Theses

4-Tier Epistemic Tagging & Falsification

Every statement is tagged as [VERIFIED FACT], [CALCULATED METRIC], [MODEL INTERPRETATION], or [UNRESOLVED UNCERTAINTY], paired with explicit quantitative filing triggers that automatically force a thesis revision.

Deep Technical Architecture & Solvers (For Quants & Engineers)
Deterministic Python Valuation Engine

Closed-form 32-iteration bisection solver for 10-year implied FCF growth rate, Graham Earning Power Value (EPV) reproduction cost asset floor, and 3-step DuPont Return on Equity decomposition (Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage).

Fixed-Income & Convexity Math

Macaulay duration, Modified duration, and second-order convexity shock matrices computed on exact cash flow schedules across SEBI ₹10k face-value debt and CCIL Sovereign Zero-Coupon Yield Curves.

Statutory Tax Waterfall (Section 115UA)

4-tier pass-through distribution categorization (Interest, Dividend, Rental Income, Capital Repayment) incorporating Finance Act Section 56(2)(xii) cost-basis amortization for REIT/InvIT unitholders.

Epistemic Grounding & Citations

Strict 4-tier truth tagging ([VERIFIED FACT], [CALCULATED METRIC], [MODEL INTERPRETATION], [UNRESOLVED UNCERTAINTY]). Multi-agent LLM analysis is restricted to qualitative footnote analysis and audit cross-checks with exact filing citations.
