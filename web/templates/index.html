Analytical Framework

The 7-Check Institutional Health Matrix

Every equity dossier is synthesized across 7 rigorous strategic dimensions grounded directly in verified BSE regulatory filings and financial disclosures.

01. Business Model & Moat

Evaluates pricing power, switching costs, network effects, and durability against low-cost disruptors.

02. Capital Allocation

Audits ROCE, ROE, reinvestment rates, dividend policy, and management stewardship of shareholder funds.

03. Financial Health & Solvency

Debt-to-equity ratios, interest coverage cushions, operating cash flow quality, and working capital traps.

04. Growth Drivers & Industry

Total addressable market (TAM), sectoral tailwinds, regulatory barriers to entry, and volume visibility.

05. Valuation & Margin of Safety

Trailing & Forward P/E multiples, EV/EBITDA bands, multi-scenario DCF estimates, and historical quartiles.

06. Technicals & Momentum

52-week price range percentiles, 50-DMA trend lines, relative volume expansion, and moving averages.

07. Governance & Forensic Cleanliness

Related party transactions, promoter share pledging, auditor qualification records, and insider SAST filings.
