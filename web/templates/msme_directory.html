MSMED Act 2020 Classification Norms (Gazette Notification S.O. 2119(E))

Micro Enterprise:
Plant & Machinery / Equipment Investment not exceeding ₹1.00 Crore and Annual Turnover not exceeding ₹5.00 Crores.
Small Enterprise:
Investment in Plant & Machinery not exceeding ₹10.00 Crores and Annual Turnover not exceeding ₹50.00 Crores.
Medium Enterprise:
Investment in Plant & Machinery not exceeding ₹50.00 Crores and Annual Turnover not exceeding ₹250.00 Crores.
