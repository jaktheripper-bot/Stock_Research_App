Frequently Asked Questions

What are Research Credits?

A research credit is used to unlock full detailed reports or synthesize fresh reports that haven't yet been generated. Reading existing reports and checking market quotes is always free.

How does payment and delivery work?

Payments are processed securely via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking, or Cards through Razorpay. Credits are added to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed. An electronic transaction receipt is generated automatically.

Can I get a refund if I don't use my credits?

Yes. Under our 7-Day Refund Policy, if you purchased an on-demand credit pack and have not consumed any of the credits, you are eligible for a 100% refund. Once a credit is consumed to generate a report, that credit is non-refundable.
