Frequently Asked Questions

What are Research Credits?

A research credit is used when you generate a fresh report from the latest BSE filings. One credit generates one report. Reading existing reports and checking quotes is always free.

How does payment and delivery work?

Payments are processed securely via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking, or Cards through Razorpay. Credits are delivered to your online account instantly within 2 seconds upon payment confirmation. An electronic transaction receipt is generated automatically.

Can I get a refund if I don't use my credits?

Yes. Under our 7-Day Refund Policy, if you purchased an on-demand credit pack and have not consumed any of the credits, you are eligible for a 100% refund. Once a credit is consumed to generate a report, that specific compute unit is non-refundable.
